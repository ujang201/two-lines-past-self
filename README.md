Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes a two-line warning to `/output/entry.txt`: line one warns the writer's past self about one specific moment — a Tuesday call from a hospital payphone, and a visit put off until Saturday — and line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what the delay cost. The text is fixed and deterministic, validated against the gate (1–280 characters, exactly two lines, one sentence each) before it is written.

Run it with `node main.ts` (Node 22.18+).
