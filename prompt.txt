Write a two-line warning to your past self for the make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self". Line one must warn about one specific, concrete moment in a single sentence — name a time, a place, and a decision. Line two must be one sentence saying why that moment mattered and what changed because of it, tracing real cause and effect rather than restating the warning. Keep the whole entry between 1 and 280 characters, plain and vivid, written to one specific person, with no wasted words. Pick a moment with a sharp, irreversible consequence — for example a phone call from a hospital payphone on a Tuesday, and a visit promised for Saturday that came too late — and let the second line land what the delay cost.
